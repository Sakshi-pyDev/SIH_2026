🔬 SIF Precursor Detection Engine
  • Analyzes near-miss and unsafe observation narratives in real time.
  • Combines TF-IDF feature weights with high-energy hazard indicators (gas kick, pressure surge, cable fraying).
  • Calculates SIF risk probability score with high-confidence alert triggers.
  • Provides explainable SHAP contribution chips for auditing.
👷 Role-Based Workflow
  • Field Workers / Drill Crew: Direct observation logging with real-time text autocomplete assistance.
  • Safety Officers / Auditors: Full oversight telemetry, risk trend monitoring, and compliance reviews.
  • System Administrators: Global system audit, user credentials, and model metrics.
⚖️ Regulatory Alignment
  • DGMS General Regulations 1984: Mandatory accident and near-miss record keeping.
  • OISD-156: Standardized Safety Management Systems for petroleum installations.
  • Petroleum Rules 2002: Safe pipeline transportation & storage protocols.
  • Encrypted role-based database storage with audit logs.
