🔬 About SIF Precursor Detection
  • Identifies near-miss and hazard reports that are precursors to Serious Injuries or Fatalities
  • NLP + TF-IDF text feature extraction combined with domain hazard-flag rules
  • SHAP values explain which features drove each risk classification
  • Trained on Oil India Limited field safety report data (2024-2026)
  • Role-based access: Field Workers submit, Officers and Admins review
👷 Who Uses This System
  • Field Worker: Submit incident, near-miss, and hazard reports directly from field
  • Safety Officer: Review all submitted reports, AI predictions and risk telemetry
  • Administrator: Full access — manage users and view all system data
  • Credentials are assigned by your Safety Department
⚖ Regulatory Compliance
  • DGMS General Regulations, 1984
  • OISD-156 Safety Management System
  • Petroleum Act 1934 & Rules 2002
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) enforced
  • All data stored securely in auditable SQLite database

ℹ Filing Guidelines
  • Report immediately — do not delay
  • Be specific about location, equipment, and persons involved
  • Describe what you observed, not assumed
  • Include precursor signals: unusual sounds, smells, vibrations
  • Reports are confidential under whistleblower policy
  • AI classification is advisory only — officer review required
